Virtual characters and avatars look lifeless when their gestures don't match the emotion in their voice. Speech2AffectiveGestures is a generative adversarial network that turns speech into 3D upper-body gestures whose emotional expression matches the speaker. Compared to the best existing methods, it reduced joint position error by **10–33%**, and study participants rated its gestures as more plausible and better matched to the emotion in the speech.

## Technical Approach
- **Generator.** Separate encoders turn the speech audio (Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients) and its text transcript into features that capture sentiment, which are combined with seed poses to synthesize a gesture sequence.
- **Affective encoder.** Multi-scale spatial-temporal graph convolutions turn 3D pose sequences into pose-based emotional features, guiding the generator toward expressive gestures.
- **Discriminator.** The same affective encoder helps the discriminator tell real gestures from synthesized ones, pushing the generator to produce gestures with the right emotional expression.

## Results
Evaluated on the TED Gesture and GENEA Challenge 2020 datasets against the best baselines:
- Mean absolute joint error reduced by **10–33%**
- Mean acceleration difference reduced by **8–58%**
- Fréchet Gesture Distance reduced by **21–34%**
- In a user study, around 15% of participants rated its gestures as more plausible than the best baseline's, and around 16% felt their emotional expression better matched the speech
